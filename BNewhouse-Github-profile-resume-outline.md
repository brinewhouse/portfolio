## 🛠️ Portfolio Architecture

Two hubs of evidence, converging into one venture thesis.

### 🏡 Homestead Hub — Permaculture & Infrastructure
Earth building, water security, and the capital model that funds it — proof of physical systems design and delivery.
- **Earth building schematics** — 3–4 structures built, with materials, labor, and outcome per project
- **Water infrastructure & security** — 3–4 installations (catchment, cisterns, wells, distribution)
- **Workshop capital sequencing** — the funding model used to pay for build phases via workshops; reusable for any future project of this kind
- Three flagships live now, telling one continuous story:
  1. [**Art of Living Ashram Phase I**](./aol-ashram-phase1.html), Bangalore — the origin story. Real 2013 installation (7 acres, 100+m swales, 250+ trees, contour gardens, adobe classroom), designed and installed by the Panya team, immediately followed by the first Permaville training course.
  2. [**Panya Project**](./panya-project.html), Thailand — the execution depth behind it. Three built systems (humanure toilets, pond liner, multi-barrier water filter) with real budgets, plus two more documented (greywater, the Sala earthen building).
  3. [**Amudham**](./amudham-methodology.html), Tamil Nadu — the methodology showcase. A theoretical ¼-acre design (clearly labeled as never built) demonstrating the full Permaville process: elements, systems specs, phased budget, staffing, and a crowdfunding capital model.
*(In progress — see `/homestead-hub` repo.)*

### 🌾 Farm Hub — Agricultural ERP & Bio-Production
Farm production models, a SQL succession database, and Tableau yield forecasting — proof of data and operations literacy.
- **Site maps** — ~2 dozen properties, varying levels of associated data detail
- **SQL succession database** — crop, planting, and harvest data normalized across farms
- **Tableau yield forecasts** — to be extrapolated further during the MBA (SQL/Tableau coursework feeds this directly)
- Flagship case study live now: **a market farm, Southeast US** — [2016 operations analysis](./se-farm-2016-analysis.html), [SQL queries](./se-farm_queries.sql), [database](./se-farm.db) *(farm de-identified by request; all data is Brian's own operational records)*
*(In progress — see `/farm-hub` repo.)*

### 👹 Commercialization Pivot — Compost Monster
Where the two hubs converge: the fulcrum between waste and food. Centered on two physical units — a biodigester and a hydroponic installation — with multiple revenue points along the corridor (compost products, hydroponic produce, install & maintenance contracts, tea/beverage line). [View the concept page](./compost-monster.html)

### 🌱 Underlying Methodology: Permaville
The published framework both hubs run on: a replicable approach to site analysis, off-grid systems design, and phased infrastructure build-out — handbook, course (5,000+ students), and policy framework (Permaville Act).
