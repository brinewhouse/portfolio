Why This One Matters

Every other Homestead Hub project is a real build with real constraints and real compromises. Amudham is the opposite and just as valuable: a clean-slate design showing the complete Permaville methodology with nothing left implicit — physical elements, systems engineering, phased budget, staffing model, and a funding mechanism, all in one document.
